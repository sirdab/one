# Timeline

Not rendered. The gallery page shows; this file explains.

One log primitive for the settings activity log, an order's history and a
customer's thread. A day is a quiet heading over one card of rows; rows are
`Item`s divided by the group's own hairlines.

- The consumer formats everything: the day heading ("Today") and the clock
  time ("09:40") arrive as text. Never relative times.
- `tone` colours the glyph only, through its `-glyph` step. Use `critical`
  for the one row that needs the owner (a refund not confirmed).
- `children` is the detail well (a message body). With `asChild` the row is
  your link, `children` is the anchor and the well goes in `detail`.
- A day split across two pages renders twice with the same heading.
