## Doctrine

Our neutral stack, built from adjudicated decisions on shadcn
primitives. Everything here renders in both themes and both
directions. Flip the toggles and make sure nothing breaks.

Ground rules, condensed:

- One primary button per view; there are no red buttons.
- Borders separate, shadows float; the page card wears the card shadow
  once and everything inside it is a hairline.
- Type comes from roles; numerals go through numerals.ts.
- The page title outranks everything else on the page.
